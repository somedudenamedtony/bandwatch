How it works

  • Your location becomes a 4-character Maidenhead grid square (about 70 × 100 mi). The app asks PSK Reporter for every reception report in that square during the window you picked.
  • Stations in the area being heard counts how many distinct stations are hearing transmitters located in the square. This is the best way to tell which bands are open from there.
  • What stations in the area hear flips the direction: it shows what receivers in the square are decoding.
  • PSK Reporter asks clients to query no more than once every 5 minutes, so results are cached and Refresh unlocks after that.
